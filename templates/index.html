Real lease data is exactly what you can't use for testing

Rent rolls, abstracts, and lease comps are locked behind confidentiality agreements — for good reason. But analytics and ML teams still need volume: hundreds of lease durations, rent escalations, and tenant mixes to stress-test a model against. LeaseSynth AI closes that gap with data that behaves like a real portfolio, built entirely from configurable statistical rules and generative AI — never from an actual tenant, property, or agreement.

Everything you need to stand up a test dataset
